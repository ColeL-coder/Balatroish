# Phase 04 — Emotional Fantasy

## Core Fantasy
Awaken an enchanted deck, bind Sigils, and weave celestial fate into score cascades.

## Target Arc
- Early: discovery, mystery, direction
- Mid: authorship, control, elegance
- Late: spectacle, inevitability, crafted power

## Tone Guardrails
- arcane luxury over chaotic noise
- effects must teach outcomes
- readability is always part of fantasy
