# Balatroish — Phase 12 Economy Principles

## Goals
Reward skill/risk, preserve meaningful choices, prevent autopilot buying.

## Sources
Round clears, excess thresholds, perfect-play bonuses, objectives, boss rewards, selling, dismantling, milestones, corruption gambles.

## Sinks
Sigils, packs, rerolls, awakenings, cleansing, slot binds, weaving, boss wards, deck editing.

## Balance Constraint
Economy must support multiple archetypes and produce persistent “one more run” tension.
